Status of these numbers

The device values are literature-range assumptions for a first exploration, not measurements of a specific fab process. Real silicon often lands 10–100× worse than simulation on energy because of control, clocking, leakage and interfaces that are not modelled.

Not modelled

  • Leakage power of ~8,000 mm² of silicon, clock distribution, control logic, host I/O, cooling.
  • Defects and redundancy repair; temperature gradients across a chip; supply noise; long-term ageing.
  • Crosstalk between neighbouring wires along their full length is only partly modelled (it was included in the exact tile solves).
  • The digital attention engine is assumed fast enough to keep up with ~1.1 µs per token per chip.
